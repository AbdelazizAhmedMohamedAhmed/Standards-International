# 20-second services video (motion graphics)

## What you get
A 20-second animated video, 16:9 (1920x1080), presenting all 7 STANDARDS services, saved to Files for review. The website stays unchanged until you approve it.

## Storyboard
1. 0–2s: STANDARDS logo reveal on deep navy, tagline "FOR INTERNATIONAL TRADE".
2. 2–18s: the 7 services, about 2.3s each: service name in large serif type, its one-line summary, a simple animated icon, and a red accent line. Real site photos (ship, warehouse, meeting, inspection, handshake) shown softly behind the text.
3. 18–20s: closing card with the logo and "Your trusted partner in international trade" style line (no invented contacts).

## Style
- Brand colors: navy #102438, racing red #D0021B, a little gold #C3A572, ivory #F8F7F4.
- Same fonts as the website; smooth, calm, premium motion.
- Music track only if available without licensing problems; otherwise silent.

## Technical details
- Service names and summaries come from the site's services list, word for word.
- Rendered by the code-animation agent using the approved logo and existing real photos as inputs; output to /mnt/documents/videos/.
- Afterwards, optionally place it on the Services page as a looping video (separate step on your approval).
